# Amirhossein Taheri

**Mobility researcher · Traffic safety · Simulation · Data and AI**

I am a PhD researcher in Automotive Engineering at Technische Universität Berlin. My work connects microscopic traffic simulation, surrogate-safety analysis, spatial methods, statistics, and machine learning to make complex mobility evidence easier to explore and use.

My research spans traffic and road safety, connected and automated vehicles, mobility analytics, emissions, and traffic-related urban heat. I am particularly interested in translating technical models into transparent research products for researchers, practitioners, and policy makers.

## Featured research products

### Mobility Safety Intelligence

An interactive research platform that turns prepared SUMO conflict outputs into interpretable safety evidence. It combines TTC-based analysis, scenario comparison, KDE hotspot ranking, interactive 3D and street-context views, LightGBM, SHAP, and literature benchmarking.

[Open the application](https://mobility-safety-intelligence.streamlit.app/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/amirhosseintaheri93-collab/mobility-safety-intelligence)

**Feature spotlights:** [Street Lens](features/street-lens.md) · [Hotspots & 3D network views](features/hotspots-3d.md) · [LightGBM + SHAP](features/explainable-ml.md) · [Mobility Mix Lab](features/mobility-mix-lab.md)

### EZSUMO

A public research demonstrator for configuring a network and policy-facing traffic assumptions through a guided interface, running SUMO in the background, and returning accessible simulation evidence. The development repository remains private while the related research is in progress.

[Open the demonstrator](https://ezsumo.streamlit.app/)

### STATS19 Calibration Map S1

An interactive supplementary map presenting police-force-area variation in model calibration across England, with explicit cautions against causal or performance-ranking interpretations.

[View the repository](https://github.com/amirhosseintaheri93-collab/stats19-calibration-map-s1)

## Methods and tools

- Traffic microsimulation: SUMO, SSAM and scenario design
- Safety analytics: TTC-based surrogate measures, crash severity and duration modelling
- Machine learning: LightGBM, SHAP, CART, Bayesian deep learning and hybrid forecasting
- Spatial analysis: KDE, hotspot ranking, geospatial transformation and interactive mapping
- Research translation: Streamlit, Python, reproducible workflows and evidence-aware interfaces

## Current directions

- Connected and automated vehicle safety across market-penetration and headway scenarios
- Accessible interfaces for simulation-based policy analysis
- Coupling traffic simulation with emissions and urban-climate modelling
- Statistical and machine-learning methods for traffic and road safety
