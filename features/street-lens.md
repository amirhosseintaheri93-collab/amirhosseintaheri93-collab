# Street Lens

The **Street Lens** places simulated conflict evidence back into its street context. Instead of reading safety results only as tables or abstract coordinates, users can explore where conflict concentrations occur relative to roads, buildings, traffic signals, crossings, public transport, water, and green areas.

## What it demonstrates

- Transformation of SUMO metric coordinates into geographic coordinates.
- Integration of prepared simulation evidence with OpenStreetMap-derived context.
- Interactive navigation between network, hotspot, and street-level views.
- Careful distinction between simulated conflict indicators and observed crashes.

## Why it matters

The lens helps researchers, practitioners, and less-technical users understand the spatial setting behind a result. It supports interpretation; it does not claim that a mapped conflict is an observed collision.
